
# Business Questions

## Overview

The purpose of the Factory Maintenance Analytics project is not simply to store maintenance data.

The objective is to use data to answer practical questions that could support maintenance and operational decision-making.

The following questions define the analytical scope of the project.

---

# 1. Equipment Reliability

## Question 1

### Which equipment fails most frequently?

The objective is to identify assets with a high number of breakdown notifications.

This can help identify equipment that may require:

- Root-cause analysis
- Additional preventive maintenance
- Replacement
- Condition monitoring

---

## Question 2

### Which equipment generates the most downtime?

High downtime can have a significant operational impact.

The analysis should identify assets responsible for the greatest number of downtime hours.

---

## Question 3

### Which equipment has the highest MTTR?

Mean Time To Repair can be used to identify assets that are difficult or time-consuming to restore.

Potential causes may include:

- Lack of spare parts
- Complex repairs
- Lack of technician availability
- Poor maintenance procedures
- Equipment design

---

## Question 4

### Which critical assets are experiencing repeated failures?

Critical equipment with frequent corrective maintenance should receive particular attention.

The analysis should combine:

- Equipment criticality
- Failure frequency
- Downtime
- Maintenance cost

---
